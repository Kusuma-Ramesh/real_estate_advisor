# india_housing_prices.csv (250,000 rows)
ID, State, City, Locality (500 ids, not city-specific), Property_Type, BHK (1-5), Size_in_SqFt (500-5000),
Price_in_Lakhs (10-500, observed), Price_per_SqFt (lakhs, rounded to 2 dp - unreliable; recomputed in code),
Year_Built (1990-2023), Furnished_Status, Floor_No, Total_Floors, Age_of_Property (= 2025 - Year_Built),
Nearby_Schools / Nearby_Hospitals (1-10), Public_Transport_Accessibility, Parking_Space, Security, Amenities,
Facing, Owner_Type, Availability_Status (Ready_to_Move / Under_Construction).
Not present: sale date, price history, future price, investment outcome, crime rate.
